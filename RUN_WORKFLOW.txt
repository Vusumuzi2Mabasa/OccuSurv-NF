VERSION 0.2 LONGITUDINAL EXTENSION
The same main.nf command also runs annual exposure simulation, start-stop QC,
time-varying cause-specific Cox models and Aalen-Johansen cumulative incidence.
The independent follow-up scenario uses seed + 1 and does not replace baseline
outcomes. Results are in longitudinal/ and longitudinal_figures/ under outdir.
Both causes require 30 complete-case events before regression is fitted.
The smoke profile explicitly exercises skipped regressions. The Word report
includes both scenarios. Equations and assumptions are in docs/METHODS.md.
Verify extension outputs with:
python validation/verify_extension.py results
python validation/verify_extension.py results-smoke
